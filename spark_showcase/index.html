My Involvement

At the beginning of my freshman year of college, I joined SPARK Electric Racing, a student-led team where we design, build, and race electric motorcycles.

Currently I am serving as SPARK's Electrical Director, where I oversee the development of all our electrical systems on the bike and collaborate with the rest of our admin team to ensure the electrical design is well-integrated with our mechanical and software systems.

Freshman Year

I began as a member of our low-voltage team, where we worked on determining requirements for our sensor system and sourced parts.

Sophomore Year

During my sophomore year, I was selected to co-lead design of our custom Battery Managment System (BMS). I focused primarily on managing our low-level software development for the project, but was involved in our hardware design as well. At the end of sophomore year, I ran for and was elected to our Electrical Director position.

Junior Year

Currently I am serving as SPARK's Electrical Director, where I oversee the development of all our electrical systems on the bike and collaborate with the rest of our admin team to ensure the electrical design is well-integrated with our mechanical and software systems.
