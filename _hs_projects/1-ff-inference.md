The development of a machine vision platform, using the NVidia Jetson Nano, was my most significant contribution while a member of my high school robotics team [*Frog Force*](https://github.com/FF503). 
During the offseason, I did extensive research on machine Learning based vision targeting. I started off by running TensorFlow Lite on a Raspberry Pi 4, and while it was functional it was not up 
to our standards for performance and accuracy. 

In the fall, I migrated our vision inferencing platform to the Jetson Nano. This shift yielded both a boost in performance and accuracy, but required a significantly different software configuration.

The Jetson Nano was used in both driver-operated periods and autonomous operation, and allowed for precise vision assisted target retrieval.
